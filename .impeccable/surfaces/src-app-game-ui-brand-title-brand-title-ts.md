# Brand mark

Scope: the Super 3T mark and every asset made from it: app header lockup, favicon (svg and ico),
apple-touch icon, web manifest icons (any and maskable) and the social preview image.
Visitor mode: persuade. It must say "this is Super 3T" at a glance, from 16 px to 1200 px.

Owner's brief (2026-11-19): not fully flat; built from the 3 and the T of "Super 3T", the 3
smaller and slightly rotated, the T bigger, drawn like the large closing marks in the win accent.
In the header too. At favicon sizes the 3T must still read (a simplified drawing is allowed).
First round: three structures; the owner picked "Fichas de salida" and asked for a better
composition than the sketch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the name is a departure board in miniature: the T is a split-flap tile that has just
landed, the 3 a smaller tile falling onto it. Refuses the category default of a letter monogram on
a flat coloured square.

OWN-WORLD: Raven Deep tile in two halves (the top half a step lighter) split by a hairline that
also cuts the T and the 3, as on a real flap. T in Apricot Flare with round caps, a halo in the
tile's own colour and the mark drop shadow, plus a faint top-edge highlight so it is not flat. The 3
in Chalk on a Raven Lift tile, rotated about -12 degrees, casting its shadow on the T. No other
colour, no gradient beyond the two flap halves.

STORY: someone sees the icon in a tab or on a home screen, reads 3T, and recognises the board
they played on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the icon square is the T's tile, full bleed. The T sits right of centre, its bar
about a third down, its stem ending near the bottom fifth. The 3's tile sits top left, tilted, its
corner resting on the left end of the T's bar. Below 48 px a simplified drawing: no 3 tile, the 3
alone in Chalk, the T without halo, both thicker.

FORM: flap tiles, position 3 on the first grounded list. seed:86d644bfe179ded6

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
